# User Feedback Summary — Aarogya Mitra

> Consent first: every quotation below was given after the person saw a written statement that (a) participation was voluntary, (b) **no real health information would be recorded**, and (c) the summary is anonymised. All quotes are fictional sample feedback used for the student-project evidence section.

## How feedback was collected

Short 10-minute usability sessions with 6 fictional pilot personas (students and one caregiver persona). Participants tried: sign-up → add medication with the safety gate → log a dose → plan a refill → invite a caregiver → revoke them. Sessions used demo data only.

## What testers valued (sample, anonymised)

1. **“It refused to guess.”** The safety gate that blocks schedule generation until the entry matches the prescription label was the single most-liked feature — testers said it *builds trust* rather than feeling like friction.
2. **Neutral reminders.** Lock-screen-safe wording (no medicine names) was called “the right privacy default”.
3. **Caregiver control.** “Revoking access instantly, and the caregiver’s screen going blank, is how it should work.”
4. **Telugu name + bilingual tone.** ఆరోగ్య మిత్ర was read as warm and local without feeling informal.

## Fructions raised

1. The add-medication form is long; testers asked for photo-of-label capture later (currently a placeholder only).
2. Voice input accuracy varied by device; testers still liked that recognised text is always shown and confirmed before saving.
3. Some wanted a calendar view of past weeks (added as /how-it-works sample).

## Measurable outcomes (sample targets, fictional)

| Metric | Before (paper diary) | After (Aarogya Mitra, sample) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Doses logged on time | ~62% | 84% of logged slots (sample) |
| Refill surprises (ran out) | 2 per month | 0 in pilot (sample) |
| Caregiver anxiety (“do I know what is happening”) | 2/5 | 4.2/5 (sample) |
| Steps to log one dose | 7 | 3 taps |

## Consent statement template used

> “You may stop at any time. We will not record your real medicines or health data — everything you enter today is sample data and will be deleted after the session. With your permission, we may quote you anonymously in our project report. Do you consent?”
